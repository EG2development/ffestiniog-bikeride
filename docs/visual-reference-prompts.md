# Nano Banana prompts — visual reference catalogue (draft)

Starts the visual reference catalogue flagged as not-yet-built in
`docs/storyboard.md` ("Known open items") and `data/source-register.md`
("Visual references (not yet gathered)"). These are **AI-generated
reference/concept images, not photographs** — label them as such wherever
used, the same way the 3D POC is labelled "Concept visualisation." They
carry Nano Banana's SynthID watermark and C2PA credentials automatically.

Grounded in the real places and facts already in this repo: the chapel in
`data/landmarks.json`, the bridge and corridor geometry in
`docs/design-3d.md` (D10, D11, POC), and the history/status facts in
`data/source-register.md`. Intended as mood-board/previz stills for the
`docs/storyboard.md` scenes and the reel's nine sections (`SECTIONS` in
`docs/design-3d.md`) — not as video. Nano Banana produces stills; animate
externally (e.g. Veo) or use the Remotion 3D pipeline for motion.

## Setup (shared by every prompt below)

- **Framework:** Text-to-Image, layered with Real-Time Search Generation
  for the named real places (chapel, lake, power station, slate quarries)
  so the model grounds form/colour in reality instead of inventing generic
  mountains.
- **Model:** Nano Banana Pro — named, real, specific locations benefit more
  from its grounding/reasoning than Nano Banana 2's speed.
- **Aspect ratio / resolution:** 16:9 at 2K, matching the reel's 1920×1080
  frame. Use 4K only for a hero/cover frame.

## 1. Overview / establishing shot (storyboard scene 1/3)

> Search for the real topography of the Vale of Ffestiniog between Blaenau
> Ffestiniog and Trawsfynydd in Snowdonia/Eryri, North Wales, then
> photograph a wide aerial establishing shot of this valley: a disused
> single-track railway corridor cutting along the valley floor between
> steep conifer-forested slopes and open upland pasture, with slate-grey
> mountains on the horizon and low morning mist pooling in the hollows.
> Camera: drone aerial, high altitude, wide 24mm-equivalent lens, slight
> downward tilt following the line of the valley, deep focus front to
> back. Lighting: soft overcast daylight breaking through, diffuse and
> cool, with a weak shaft of sun catching the valley floor. Colour
> grading: naturalistic British-landscape palette, muted greens and greys
> with a cool blue-grey sky, no heavy stylisation. Shot on a medium-format
> digital camera for clean dynamic range. 16:9, 2K.

## 2. Heritage / archival character (storyboard scene 2)

> Search for historical photographs of the Bala and Festiniog Railway
> before its 1960s closure, then create an image evoking that period: a
> narrow-gauge-scale single railway track running through a wooded Welsh
> valley cutting, timber sleepers and ballast visible, no modern trail
> surfacing, a small stone-built halt platform visible in the middle
> distance, empty of people. Camera: eye-level on the trackbed looking
> along the rails into the distance, 35mm lens, shallow depth with the
> rails sharp and the trees softly out of focus beyond. Lighting: low
> autumn sun raking along the track from one side, long shadows. Colour
> grading: desaturated sepia-toned archival still, soft grain, slightly
> faded contrast as if scanned from an old print. Materiality: weathered
> timber sleepers, rusted iron chairs, moss-covered stone abutments.
> 16:9, 2K.

## 3. Blaenau Ffestiniog — slate quarry town (storyboard scene 4)

> Search for the real appearance of Blaenau Ffestiniog's slate quarries
> and terraced town in Gwynedd, Wales, then photograph the start of the
> cycle corridor leaving the town: grey slate-roofed terraced houses
> stepping down a hillside, enormous grey slate quarry tips and exposed
> rock terraces rising behind the town, the still-operating railway
> station visible below, and the disused Bala and Festiniog trackbed
> beginning to curve away from the settlement into open country. Camera:
> elevated three-quarter view from a hillside path, 35mm lens, deep focus.
> Lighting: flat, cool, slightly overcast Welsh daylight, typical of the
> quarrying region. Colour grading: desaturated slate-grey and
> heather-purple palette with muted greens, true to the stone-dominated
> landscape, no warm stylisation. Materiality: split-face slate stone, wet
> tarmac, lichen-stained roof slates. 16:9, 2K.

## 4. Vale North — upland farmland corridor (storyboard scene 5)

> Search for the real character of upland sheep-farming country in the
> Vale of Ffestiniog near Gellilydan, Wales, then photograph the disused
> railway corridor running as a grassed trackbed between drystone walls,
> with rough grazing pasture and scattered gorse and heath either side, a
> few sheep in the middle distance, and forested hillsides rising beyond
> the fields. Camera: low eye-level shot along the trackbed, 50mm lens,
> shallow-to-moderate depth of field with the near trackbed in focus.
> Lighting: bright but hazy overcast light, even and shadowless. Colour
> grading: natural, slightly cool-toned pastoral palette — sage greens,
> straw-coloured grass, grey stone walls. Materiality: lichen-mottled
> drystone wall capstones, rutted grass trackbed, gorse in sparse yellow
> flower. 16:9, 2K.

## 5. Vale South — Capel Utica chapel near Gellilydan (storyboard scene 5)

> Search for Geograph photos 443457 and 449941 and the OpenStreetMap
> record for Capel Utica, a Welsh Independent chapel near Gellilydan,
> Gwynedd, then photograph the valley corridor passing roughly 400 metres
> from it: a gabled, slate-roofed stone chapel with a plain road-facing
> gable and porch, no tower or bellcote, standing among gravestones at the
> edge of a small settlement, seen across rough pasture and a line of
> hedgerow trees from the elevated trackbed. Camera: medium-long shot from
> the trackbed looking across the fields toward the chapel, 85mm lens,
> moderate compression, the chapel readably built-form on the horizon
> rather than isolated subject. Lighting: soft late-afternoon sun from a
> low angle, warm but not golden-hour intense. Colour grading: naturalistic
> rural Wales palette, warm stone tones against cool green fields.
> Materiality: rough-dressed grey stone walls, dark slate roof, iron
> chapel railings. 16:9, 2K.

## 6. Maentwrog Road — wooded corridor with the railway bridge (storyboard scene 6; matches the 3D POC)

> Search for the real landscape around the former Maentwrog Road railway
> station and the Bala and Festiniog trackbed at approximately kilometre 9
> of the line, then photograph a wooded cutting where the disused railway,
> now a grassed trail, passes beneath a short stone-and-brick railway
> bridge roughly twenty metres long carrying a quiet unclassified lane
> overhead, with mixed conifer and broadleaf woodland crowding both
> embankments and dappled light falling across the trail surface. Camera:
> eye-level shot on the trackbed approaching the bridge, 35mm lens, the
> bridge arch framing the view ahead, moderate depth of field. Lighting:
> filtered woodland daylight, soft dappled patches of sun through the
> canopy. Colour grading: rich woodland greens with warm highlights where
> sun breaks through, natural saturation. Materiality: weathered stone and
> brick bridge parapets, moss on the abutments, leaf-littered trail
> surface. 16:9, 2K.

## 7. Trawsfynydd — lake and former power station (storyboard scene 7)

> Search for the real appearance of Llyn Trawsfynydd and the decommissioned
> Trawsfynydd nuclear power station in Gwynedd, Wales, then photograph the
> corridor emerging onto open moorland above the lake: a wide expanse of
> still water reflecting the sky, bare upland moor and scattered conifer
> plantation around the shore, and the large flat-roofed concrete reactor
> halls of the former power station visible as a distant, low, grey
> industrial form across the water, clearly background rather than
> foreground subject. Camera: elevated three-quarter aerial view from the
> trackbed's higher ground, 24mm wide-angle lens, deep focus holding both
> the near moorland and the distant lake and power station sharp.
> Lighting: cool, slightly overcast upland light with a break of sun
> glinting on the water. Colour grading: cool slate-blue and moor-brown
> palette, restrained and documentary rather than dramatic. Materiality:
> tussocky moor grass, bare rock outcrops, still reflective lake water.
> 16:9, 2K.

## 8. Closing — dusk wide vista (storyboard scene 8)

> Search for the real skyline and light quality of Eryri/Snowdonia at dusk
> in autumn, then photograph a wide closing shot looking back down the
> valley corridor from high ground, with the disused railway trackbed
> trail curving away into the shadowed valley floor, forested slopes
> silhouetting on either side, and the sky holding the last warm light
> above the darkening mountains. Camera: elevated wide shot, 24mm lens,
> deep focus, horizon held low in the frame to emphasise the sky.
> Lighting: late dusk, warm amber afterglow fading to cool blue in the
> upper sky, long soft shadows pooling in the valley. Colour grading:
> cinematic but restrained — warm horizon, cool shadow, gentle contrast,
> no heavy teal/orange stylisation. 16:9, 2K.
